# Corporate AI Policy Template (EBF Version)
*This template is designed for small to medium-sized businesses in the UK.*

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy outlines the acceptable use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) at **[Your Business Name]**. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party partners.

## 2. Permitted Tools
*   **Approved Tools:** Only AI tools approved by the IT/Security lead may be used for business purposes (e.g., Microsoft Copilot for Enterprise, ChatGPT Enterprise).
*   **Prohibited Use:** No sensitive business or client data may be entered into public, non-enterprise versions of AI tools.

## 3. Data Privacy & UK GDPR
*   **Data Protection:** Personal Identifiable Information (PII) must never be uploaded to AI models unless the specific tool is verified to comply with UK GDPR (i.e., data is not used for training and is encrypted).
*   **DPIA:** A Data Protection Impact Assessment must be conducted before implementing any high-risk AI system.

## 4. Accuracy & Accountability
*   **Human-in-the-Loop:** All AI-generated content (contracts, emails, marketing, code) must be reviewed and approved by a qualified human expert before being used or sent to clients.
*   **Attribution:** Employees must disclose when AI has been used to generate a significant portion of a work product.
*   **Hallucinations:** We acknowledge that AI can produce false or misleading information ("hallucinations"). Verification against primary sources is mandatory.

## 5. Implementation Strategy
*   **Grounded Data:** Where possible, we use Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to ensure AI outputs are based on our own verified internal documents.
*   **Continuous Learning:** Staff will receive training on prompt engineering and AI security risks annually.

## 6. Security
*   **API Keys:** API keys and credentials for AI services must be managed securely and never committed to public repositories.
*   **Vendor Risk:** All AI vendors must undergo a security assessment to ensure their data handling practices meet our standards.

*Reference: Derived from ICO Guidance and NCSC AI Security Standards.*
